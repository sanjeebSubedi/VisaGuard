title: SEVP Policy Guidance for Directly Related Employment
---
For practical training, the employment must be directly related to the student's major area of study.

Students should be able to explain how the job duties and responsibilities are connected to the knowledge and skills gained through their degree program.

The relationship should be supported by the nature of the work performed, not solely by the employer name or job title.
