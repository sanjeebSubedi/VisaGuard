## Six-month validation reports

A student on STEM OPT must regularly validate that SEVIS still reflects the student's current circumstances.

Every six months, the student must confirm with the DSO that SEVIS correctly reflects the student's:
- legal name,
- residential or mailing address,
- employer name and address, and
- current employment status.

Students should report changes, including loss of employment, within 10 days of the change.

## Annual and final self-evaluations

A STEM OPT student must complete and submit self-evaluations describing progress in the training experience.

Required evaluations include:
- an initial evaluation 12 months after the STEM OPT start date, and
- a final evaluation at the end of the full training period or earlier if the employment ends sooner.

The student must ensure the DSO receives the required evaluation no later than 10 days after the end of the relevant reporting period or training period.

## Material changes to Form I-983

A STEM OPT student must report to the DSO any material changes to, or material deviations from, the original training plan.

Examples of material changes include:
- a change in the employer's EIN,
- a reduction in compensation that is not tied to fewer hours worked,
- a significant decrease in hours per week,
- changes to the employer's commitments, and
- changes to the student's learning objectives.

## Changing employers during STEM OPT

When a student changes employers during STEM OPT:
- the new employer must be enrolled in E-Verify before the student begins work for pay,
- the student must submit a new Form I-983 to the DSO within 10 days of starting the new training opportunity, and
- the student must submit the final self-evaluation for the previous employer within 10 days of ending that employment.

If there is a gap between employers longer than 10 days, the student must first report the loss of employment to the DSO and later submit the new Form I-983.

## Unemployment reporting

STEM OPT students must report the termination of their practical training experience within 10 days of the event.

## Useful explanation language for students

A student on STEM OPT has more reporting obligations than a student on standard OPT. The student must stay current on six-month validations, annual and final self-evaluations, and any material changes to Form I-983 or employer information.
