## Practical training overview

Practical training is employment directly related to the student's major area of study.

For F-1 students, the main practical training categories are:
- Curricular Practical Training (CPT)
- Optional Practical Training (OPT)
- STEM OPT extension

Students in English-language training programs are not eligible for practical training.

## Post-completion OPT employment rules

A student may be authorized for up to 12 months of OPT per educational level.

Post-completion OPT requires:
- a DSO recommendation entered into SEVIS,
- a properly filed Form I-765, and
- a valid EAD from USCIS.

A student may not begin post-completion OPT before the date shown on the EAD.

## Employment types relevant to students

USCIS guidance confirms that a student may work as:
- a volunteer, or
- an unpaid intern,

as long as the arrangement does not violate labor laws, is related to the student's course of study, and involves at least 20 hours of work per week for post-completion OPT.

## Grace period and unemployment

After completing an authorized period of post-completion OPT, an F-1 student may remain in F-1 status for 60 days beyond the EAD expiration date.

During post-completion OPT, a student may not accrue more than 90 days of unemployment.

During STEM OPT, a student may not accrue more than 150 total days of unemployment across post-completion OPT and STEM OPT.

## STEM OPT employer and training plan rules

A student on STEM OPT must:
- work with a qualifying employer,
- maintain a valid Form I-983 training plan,
- submit required self-evaluations, and
- report required changes to the DSO.

USCIS guidance explains that start-up employment can qualify if the employer meets the STEM OPT rules, including E-Verify participation, training-plan compliance, and bona fide employment.

## OPT reporting and filing points

For standard OPT, the student must report changes of name or address and interruptions in employment to the DSO.

For STEM OPT, the student must also:
- report key personal and employer changes within 10 days,
- complete six-month validations,
- submit required Form I-983 evaluations, and
- provide a new or modified Form I-983 when required.

## Useful explanation language for students

A student's job arrangement can be valid under OPT in several different forms, but the work must still be directly related to the degree and satisfy the weekly work-hour and reporting rules that apply to the student's OPT category.
