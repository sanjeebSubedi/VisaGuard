## Core eligibility rules

A student may qualify for a 24-month STEM OPT extension only if the student is currently in a period of post-completion OPT. A student may not apply during the 60-day grace period after the initial OPT period.

A student may receive a STEM OPT extension:
- once per degree level, and
- no more than twice across the student's academic career if the student completes qualifying degrees at different educational levels.

## Degree requirements

The qualifying degree must be:
- at the bachelor's level or higher,
- in a field on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List, and
- from an SEVP-certified school that is accredited at the time the STEM OPT application is filed.

A prior STEM degree may be used in some cases if:
- the most recent degree came from a currently accredited SEVP-certified school,
- the prior STEM degree was earned within 10 years before the STEM OPT filing date, and
- the prior degree is on the STEM Designated Degree Program List at the time of filing.

## Employer requirements

To support a STEM OPT extension, the employer must:
- be enrolled in E-Verify,
- work with the student to complete and sign Form I-983,
- provide a training opportunity that has a direct relationship to the student's qualifying STEM degree, and
- offer at least 20 hours of work per week during the STEM OPT extension.

A student cannot qualify unless the student is a bona fide employee of the employer signing Form I-983. The student cannot provide the employer attestation on their own behalf.

A start-up business may qualify if all STEM OPT regulatory requirements are met and the employer that signs Form I-983 is the same entity that actually employs the student and provides the training experience.

## DSO-specific review points

Before recommending STEM OPT, a DSO should confirm:
- the student is in active F-1 status and in an authorized period of post-completion OPT,
- the student is not applying during the grace period,
- the degree qualifies under the STEM list and accreditation rules,
- the prospective employer is in E-Verify, and
- Form I-983 is properly completed and signed.

## Useful explanation language for students

A student can be told that STEM OPT depends on both the degree and the job setup. Even if the degree qualifies, the extension still requires a qualifying employer, a properly completed Form I-983, and a training role that is directly related to the qualifying STEM degree.
