## What practical training is

Practical training for an F-1 student must be directly related to the student's major area of study.

There are two main practical training paths relevant here:
- Curricular practical training (CPT), which is authorized by the DSO and must be an integral part of the curriculum.
- Optional practical training (OPT), which is recommended by the DSO and authorized by USCIS.

An eligible F-1 student may receive up to 12 months of OPT per educational level. A student may become eligible for another 12 months after moving to a higher educational level.

## Post-completion OPT basics

A student may apply for post-completion OPT:
- up to 90 days before the program end date, and
- no later than 60 days after the program end date.

The student must also file with USCIS within 30 days of the date the DSO enters the OPT recommendation into SEVIS.

A student may not begin OPT before the approved start date on the EAD. The requested OPT start date cannot be more than 60 days after the program end date.

## DSO recommendation and student responsibilities

To apply for OPT, the student must first request an OPT recommendation from the DSO. The DSO must update SEVIS and issue a signed Form I-20 reflecting the recommendation.

Once a DSO recommends OPT, the school is responsible for maintaining the student's SEVIS record during the entire period of authorized OPT.

## Reporting during OPT

During OPT, the student must report to the DSO any:
- change of legal name,
- change of address, or
- interruption of employment.

The DSO must update SEVIS to reflect those reported changes for the duration of authorized OPT.

## Additional STEM OPT reporting rules

For a 24-month STEM OPT extension, the student must also:
- report a change of legal name, residential or mailing address, employer name, employer address, or loss of employment within 10 days of the change; and
- complete a validation report every six months, due within 10 business days of each reporting date.

## Grace period after study or OPT

After completing a course of study and any authorized practical training following completion of studies, an F-1 student is allowed an additional 60-day period to prepare for departure, transfer, or otherwise take a lawful next step.

A student who fails to maintain status is not eligible for this additional departure period.

## Travel and temporary absence while on OPT

An F-1 student with an unexpired EAD for post-completion OPT may return to the United States after a temporary trip abroad if the student is otherwise admissible.

The EAD must be used together with a Form I-20 endorsed for reentry by the DSO within the last six months.

## Cap-gap extension rule

If an F-1 student is the beneficiary of a timely filed, nonfrivolous H-1B petition requesting change of status for the next fiscal year, the student's duration of status and certain employment authorization may automatically extend until April 1 of that fiscal year or the petition validity start date, whichever is earlier.
