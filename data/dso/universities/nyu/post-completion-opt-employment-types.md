## NYU's main rule summary

NYU states that all post-completion OPT employment must:
- be related to the student's degree program, and
- average at least 20 hours per week.

The page notes that the listed employment options do not apply to STEM OPT unless separately stated.

## Employment types NYU says can count for post-completion OPT

### Paid employment

A student on post-completion OPT may work part-time or full-time, as long as the total employment averages at least 20 hours per week.

### Multiple employers

A student may work for more than one employer, but all employment must be related to the degree program.

### Short-term multiple employers or gig work

NYU says a student may work for multiple short-term employers or gigs. Each gig should be reported separately with the employer name and employer address.

The student should keep records showing:
- dates,
- hours, and
- job description.

NYU notes that gig work is generally not treated as self-employment unless the student has a proper business license.

### Work for hire

NYU explains that work for hire means work performed under a contractual relationship rather than a traditional employment relationship. The student should be prepared to show evidence of the contract duration and the contracting company name and address.

### Self-employed business owner

NYU says a student on OPT may start a business and be self-employed. In that situation, the student must:
- work full time,
- have the proper business licenses,
- be actively engaged in a business related to the degree program, and
- be able to document the arrangement.

### Employment through an agency

A student working through an agency must still be able to provide evidence that the student worked at least 20 hours per week.

### Unpaid employment

NYU says a student may work as a volunteer or unpaid intern so long as the arrangement does not violate labor laws. The work must be at least 20 hours per week for post-completion OPT, and the student must be able to provide evidence from the employer showing the weekly hours worked.

## Useful explanation language for students

At NYU, a student can have more than one OPT job or even certain forms of gig, contract, self-employed, agency, or unpaid work, but every role must still be related to the degree and the student must keep enough records to prove the work qualifies.
