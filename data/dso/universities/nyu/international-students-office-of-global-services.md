## Main NYU contact guidance

NYU tells students with questions about:
- student visas,
- immigration,
- Form I-20,
- travel planning, or
- employment-related immigration matters

to contact the Office of Global Services (OGS).

## Student-facing explanation

For NYU-specific immigration procedures, the DSO copilot should direct students to OGS when the question is about school process, internal handling, or next steps involving university support.
